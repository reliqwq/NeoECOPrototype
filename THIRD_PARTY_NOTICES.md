Development JARs are resolved from local files, so a fresh clone cannot compile until they are placed; the build then stops with the exact missing paths instead of failing later as unresolved symbols. The `21.3.0` release of Neo ECO AE Extension used as the baseline is published upstream (GitHub release and Modrinth); it stays a local file here - a packaging note, not a stability one.

Data Energistics is in that table because it is the only AGPL upstream we compile against, and the link is deliberately narrow. Measured on `neoecoprototype-1.4.10.jar`: exactly **one** shipped class names `com/fish_dan_` - `integration/dataenergistics/MusicDataEnergisticsPlugin`, which implements DE's published `api.entrypoint.DataEnergisticsPlugin` and registers one AE key type through `api.registry.digitalsupply`. Every DE reference in it sits inside DE's own `api/` packages; no `common/**` internal is named by anything we distribute, and the jar contains **0 entries under the `data_energistics` namespace and 0 dependency JARs**. The one shipped data file that mentions a `data_energistics:` id - `data/neoecoprototype/recipe/data_reassembler/data_flow_from_music_data.json` - is our own recipe naming their item id, which is interop rather than reuse of their files, and their resource license (separate from the code license, and All Rights Reserved) is the reason that distinction is written down. The gametest probe at `src/main/java/cn/dancingsnow/neoecoprototype/gametest/DataEnergisticsTestProbe.java` does import `common/**` types, and is therefore kept out of the release artifact (measured: 0 `gametest` classes in the jar above).

The reason this is recorded instead of assumed: implementing an extension interface from our own source does not make our jar a derivative work of theirs, but lifting their code into `src/` would, and then the whole artifact would have to be conveyed under AGPL-3.0-or-later with its section 13 remote-service terms - which our `GPL-3.0-only` declaration cannot carry. That is the line route D of the wireless channel work stays behind: its `6 x 32` port arithmetic is derived from AE2's own public constants (a dense cable is 32, a cube has 6 faces) and written here from scratch; DE's implementation was read off its bytecode for shape, the same way eco's and AE2's are, and nothing of it was copied. See docs/wireless-channel-ceiling.md.

The upstream mod's own metadata lists its authors as DancingSnow, ZhuRuoLing and Yang120231. Special thanks to `Yang120` for substantial help from the original Neo ECO AE Extension team.

One model, not a texture, is copied rather than referenced: `models/block/simplify_casing.json` carries the 13-element geometry of upstream `assets/neoecoae/models/block/casing_base.json` with its own texture slots. It is inlined on purpose - this addon's CI rejects any asset that points into the `neoecoae` namespace, because an upstream rename would otherwise turn our casing into a silent missing model. Geometry only; the surface artwork is our own.

The earlier repository-generated coolant tiles and the PR #17 sundae glass were removed from the active tree and survive only in local backups under `_removed_textures/`; no AI generation or pixel copying was used for the files that ship now. **All Rights Reserved (ARR)**: not to be extracted, reused or redistributed separately without permission. Permission to use the artwork as part of NeoECOPrototype does not transfer ownership or grant a separate redistribution license.
2. **28 adapted from Neo ECO AE Extension** - 20 match an eco texture under the signature ruler (9 pixel-identical copies and 11 same-layout palette variants), and 8 are placed by provenance: five cell-light variants (`eco_cell_light_{1k,16k,64k,1m,4m_still}.png`) whose current layouts differ from eco's tiles by one or more pixels, the two workstation-panel crops, and the workstation module card. The panel crops and card are generated by `tools/make_processor_assembly_panel_icons.py` from eco's OTHER column and empty-module tile; their grey bodies are retained and the blue accents are moved onto our green pair. The six former workstation-controller copies and retints are no longer in the active tree. Upstream publishes under GPL-3.0 with no separate asset clause, so these carry upstream's licensing and attribution requirements and are not relicensed by this addon.
3. **18 adapted from Applied Energistics 2** - 2 pixel-identical copies and 16 same-layout palette variants: three pattern-provider faces (the fourth, `green_pattern_provider_alternate_front.png`, survives only in the legacy pack), one interface face, three part backs, the four budding Grette mother rocks, the solid Grette crystal block, the Grette crystal dust, and the two ME-chest bottom/top pairs plus the energized energy-cell face added with the chest work. AE2's asset notice reads, verbatim: *"Textures and Models (c) 2020, Ridanisaurus Rid, (c) 2013 - 2020 AlgorithmX2 et al / License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0"*. That license requires attribution, carries a non-commercial term, and requires derivatives to be shared under the same terms. **This addon makes no ARR claim over those eighteen files**, and anyone reusing them follows AE2's terms rather than ours.

The Grette-family AE2-shaped tiles keep AE2's pixel layout and take their colours from our own `grette_cluster.png`, which is the family's colour authority: AE2's nine-colour ramp is first darkened (hue 210, saturation x1.55, value x0.72, saturation floor 0.12), then its hue and saturation are re-skinned along the cluster's own luminance curve, AE2's value ladder untouched. Measured with the mapping-break ruler, comparing only pixels opaque in both tiles: **0 of the comparable pixels break the colour mapping** for each tile against its own AE2 source - 0/256 for the mother rocks and the block, 0/88 for the dust, whose remaining pixels are AE2's colour-under-alpha. They replace an original drawing by our lead artist; the previous tiles are kept outside the repository for comparison. Adopting AE2's shape also gives up one property our own drawing had: our four conditions shared one stone body and changed only a 9x8 centre box (36-42 pixels, 0 outside), while these change 29-56 pixels spread over x3-12 / y2-12, leaving 195 of 256 pixels identical across all four.

The counts above were re-derived on 2026-11-27 by file content against the two jars this addon builds against - `ae2-19.2.17.jar` (614 textures indexed) and `neoecoae-21.3.0.jar` (476). A tile's signature is its size, nontransparent colour count and per-pixel colour-equality map; matching signatures at equal size with at least three colours means "same layout, different palette", while exact palette matches also count flat sheets. The active tree is **336 = 290 original + 28 eco-derived + 18 AE2-derived**. The ruler matches **21 eco-shaped and 19 AE2-shaped** active tiles; the crystal block matches both and is counted once in group 3, leaving 20 eco-shaped tiles in group 2, and the fully transparent energy-cell light sheet matches both and is counted once in group 2. The other eight group-2 tiles are placed by provenance as described above. The index includes tiles taller than 64px, because dropping them undercounts the animated cell art; transparent pixels are excluded from the equality map so AE2's colour-under-alpha is not reported as a broken palette mapping. This supersedes the 2026-11-12 census against eco 21.2.16.

The jar ships more PNGs than this census counts: **484 in total**. `legacy_art/` is a built-in fallback resource pack re-cut from a released tag by `tools/legacy_pack.py` - **144 PNGs (143 textures plus the pack icon)** that a player can switch on from the resource-pack screen. Measured against the same two jars, **65** of those textures reproduce an eco texture pixel for pixel, **44** keep eco's layout with another palette, **0** reproduce an AE2 texture pixel for pixel, **4** keep AE2's layout, and **30** carry no upstream signature under this ruler - **4** of those have fewer than three colours, which the ruler refuses rather than match by accident. The fully transparent cell-light sheet is counted with eco, not twice. Every matched legacy texture stays under the same upstream terms as group 2 or 3. Three GUI tiles ship in the AE2 namespace (`assets/ae2/textures/guis/neoecoprototype/{l1_pattern_provider,l1_powered_me_interface,superconductive_interface}.png`) because AE2's screen loader resolves them from there; they are our own drawings and match no jar texture. `pack.png` and `logo.png` are icons, not textures.

## Player skins bundled for the named dolls

`textures/block/dolls/{reliqwq,yang120,kouooki,tedxenon}.png` are the actual player skins of four named people, shipped so their dolls show the right face without reaching the session service. A player skin is that player's own content, and Mojang's usage terms do not license us to redistribute it, so each of these four files stands on that person's own say-so. **Adding another doll's face requires asking first, not fetching.**
