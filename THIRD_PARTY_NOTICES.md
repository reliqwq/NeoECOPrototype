Development JARs are resolved from local files, so a fresh clone cannot compile until they are placed; the build then stops with the exact missing paths instead of failing later as unresolved symbols. The `21.2.9` build of Neo ECO AE Extension used as the baseline was handed over by its author and has no public download page yet - a packaging note, not a stability one.

The upstream mod's own metadata lists its authors as DancingSnow, ZhuRuoLing and Yang120231. Special thanks to `Yang120` for substantial help from the original Neo ECO AE Extension team.

One model, not a texture, is copied rather than referenced: `models/block/simplify_casing.json` carries the 13-element geometry of upstream `assets/neoecoae/models/block/casing_base.json` with its own texture slots. It is inlined on purpose - this addon's CI rejects any asset that points into the `neoecoae` namespace, because an upstream rename would otherwise turn our casing into a silent missing model. Geometry only; the surface artwork is our own.

The tile has 13 colours: **7 of eco's 10** crystal-ramp colours (`cryotheum_crystal.png` is a 12-frame animation and all twelve frames use the same ten) and **6 of its own** - `(74,95,110)` slate, `(185,211,222)`, `(217,210,191)`, `(242,250,255)`, `(247,243,232)` and plain white. Five of those six appear in **none of the other 336 texture PNGs this addon ships**; white is in 19 of ours and 118 of the 1425 PNGs in the two jars we build against, and the slate appears in exactly one jar file - AE2's `ae2guide/assets/diagrams/quantum_bridge_demonstration.png`, a 1920×1057 guide illustration, not a pixel tile - so it is not borrowed from anyone's pixel art either. Per pixel against eco's crystal frame 0, **5 of its 126 opaque pixels share both position and colour**, so it is not a recolour and does not belong in group 2. No measurement here can establish how it was made, or what the generator was trained on; "original layout, no pixels copied" is the contributor's claim, and the numbers above are the check on the part of it we can check. Two deviations from the family are known and were accepted on purpose when this one was chosen over the four candidates that keep eco's outline: **the dominant outline is that slate `(74,95,110)` rather than eco's deep-sea blue `(12,63,104)`**, and the cream highlights `(247,243,232)` / `(217,210,191)` are new to the cryotheum family. We ship it under our own ARR notice because the contributor offered it that way, and anyone who takes a different view of AI-generated work should treat that one file as the first to remove.

The AE2-shaped tiles keep AE2's pixel layout and take their colours from our own `grette_cluster.png`, which is the family's colour authority: AE2's nine-colour ramp is first darkened (hue 210, saturation x1.55, value x0.72, saturation floor 0.12), then its hue and saturation are re-skinned along the cluster's own luminance curve, AE2's value ladder untouched. Measured with the mapping-break ruler, comparing only pixels opaque in both tiles: **0 of the comparable pixels break the colour mapping** for each tile against its own AE2 source - 0/256 for the mother rocks and the block, 0/88 for the dust, whose remaining pixels are AE2's colour-under-alpha. They replace an original drawing by our lead artist; the previous tiles are kept outside the repository for comparison. Adopting AE2's shape also gives up one property our own drawing had: our four conditions shared one stone body and changed only a 9x8 centre box (36-42 pixels, 0 outside), while these change 29-56 pixels spread over x3-12 / y2-12, leaving 195 of 256 pixels identical across all four.

The counts above were re-derived on 2026-11-04 by file content, not file name, against the two jars this addon actually builds against - `ae2-19.2.17.jar` (614 textures indexed) and `neoecoae-21.2.15-beta.jar` (480). A tile's signature is its size, its colour count and its per-pixel colour-equality map, which a pure recolour preserves exactly; matching signatures at equal size with at least three distinct colours is what "same layout, different palette" means here, so flat glow sheets cannot match by accident. The split came out **50 from eco + 14 from AE2 = 64** re-run on 2026-11-05 after the reward-bag tile was added: still 50 + 14 = 64 derived out of 329 (326 then three new tiles, all of them group 1), and the new tile - a darkened copy of a group-1 tile - matches no upstream texture, so it stays in group 1. Re-derived once more on 2026-11-07 after `sky_stone_dust.png` retired and the workstation-card retint arrived: still **50 + 14 = 64 out of 329**, because one group-1 tile went out and one came in, and the retint matches no signature in `ae2-19.2.17.jar` or `neoecoae-21.2.15.jar` either. Re-derived a second time on 2026-11-07 for the two workstation-panel sprites, which at that point were our own drawing: still **50 + 14 = 64 out of 331**, since neither sprite had a same-size counterpart in either jar and each was coloured from its own ramp (0 of 6 and 0 of 5 of their colours appear in AE2's 655 PNGs; the single colour they share with eco's 771 is our own L1 tier green). Later the same day they were replaced by crops of eco's OTHER column, as above, which moves them into group 2 and makes the file total 337. Re-derived a third time on 2026-11-07 for the six formed-controller textures: **54 + 14 = 68 out of 337** - measured, not carried over. The three copies are pixel-identical to their eco sources at equal size (128x128, 16x16, 16x16). Of the three recolours, `controller_screen_on.png` is a pure palette map (16x352, every one of its 22 frames) and still matches its source's colour-equality map exactly. The two light sheets (both 128x256 two-frame strips; frame 0 of each is the retinted eco sheet, and the idle one's second frame flips both greens on its front and top faces) retint only the north and up faces: the same source blue appears both retinted there and untouched on the west/south/east faces, so their per-pixel colour-equality map necessarily differs from the source and this ruler does not count them - they join group 2 by layout and provenance instead. The two workstation-panel sprites sit outside the count as well, having no same-size counterpart in either jar. That is 58 group-2 tiles minus the four the ruler cannot count, so the signature total is four below group 2's 58. The same re-run is what moved the slate `(74,95,110)` back to appearing in one file only, the hand-drawn bag tile having been replaced, and the 50 reproducing the earlier audit's eco figure against a different jar is the check that this is the same method. Two ways this measurement goes wrong are recorded because both were actually hit: an index that skips tiles taller than 64px drops precisely the animated cell art counted as byte-for-byte copies and reports 43 instead of 50; and a mapping-break ruler that counts transparent pixels reads AE2's colour-under-alpha as a broken mapping, scoring a pure palette map 29/256 where the honest figure is 0/88. That ruler is also lenient when the candidate has very few colours, which is why `sky_stone_dust.png` (3 colours) looked like a recolour of AE2's `sky_dust.png` (13 colours) under it and is not one under this rule - its equality structure differs, so it stays in group 1. The colours of the 2026-11-04 tiles come from our own `grette_cluster.png`: the crystal item's off-family colours snap onto the cluster's band by an injective, order-preserving assignment, and the AE2-shaped tiles take hue and saturation from that same band while keeping AE2's own value ladder. The project code remains GPL-3.0-only, and GPL does not apply to the ARR artwork in group 1.

## Player skins bundled for the named dolls

`textures/block/dolls/{reliqwq,yang120,kouooki,tedxenon}.png` are the actual player skins of four named people, shipped so their dolls show the right face without reaching the session service. A player skin is that player's own content, and Mojang's usage terms do not license us to redistribute it, so each of these four files stands on that person's own say-so. **Adding another doll's face requires asking first, not fetching.**
